> Privacy & Policy

Otan ID · Last updated: November 20, 2026

What we store

  • Your username.
  • Your password, only as a salted hash. We cannot read it.
  • Your 12-digit value, only as a keyed hash. We cannot read or restore it.
  • Dates of account creation and last log in.
  • Active sessions: a hashed session token and its expiry.
  • A keyed hash of your IP address, used only to limit failed attempts and removed automatically after 48 hours.

What we don't

No email, phone number or real name. No advertising, no tracking cookies. Your session token is kept in your browser's local storage on this device.

Your value

The value is shown once, when you sign up. You need it to log in and to reset your username or password. If you lose it, the account cannot be recovered. Anyone who has your value can reset your account, so do not share it.

Sessions and attempts

A session lasts 7 days, then you log in again. After 5 failed attempts, further attempts are blocked for 24 hours.

Deleting your data

Use Delete in the top bar at any time: your account and sessions are erased permanently. An account that has not logged in for 30 days is deleted automatically.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. Data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) and the site is served by Vercel.
