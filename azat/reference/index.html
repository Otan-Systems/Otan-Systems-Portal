Section 1
Core Philosophy & Architecture

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of data within Azat AI, a specialised artificial intelligence language model application operated by Otan Systems. Azat AI acts as a gateway: it relays your prompts to third-party AI infrastructure using an API key that you provide.

Architecture
Pass-Through Gateway
Your prompts are relayed to the AI provider; conversation content is not stored on our servers.
Data Sales
Never
We do not sell, rent, or trade user information with any third party.
Guiding Principle
Minimum Necessary
Only what is strictly required to deliver the service — nothing more.

By authenticating and using Azat AI, you acknowledge and agree to the data practices outlined in this Policy.

Section 2
Data We Do Not Collect

Azat AI keeps only a minimal account record (described in Section 3). The following data is never collected or stored:

  • Names, email addresses, or phone numbers (we store only Google's account identifier)
  • User passwords of any kind
  • Payment card, bank, or crypto wallet data (Azat AI does not process payments)
  • Identity documents or biometric data
  • Passports or driver's licences (Zero KYC)
  • Web analytics or behavioural tracking
  • Advertising pixels (Meta, TikTok, etc.)
  • Tracking cookies of any type

Section 3
Data Processing & Authentication
3.1 Authentication Layer

Access is managed via a trusted third-party OAuth 2.0 provider. Supported method:

Provider
Continue with Google
How it works: During login, authentication occurs on Google's secure infrastructure. Otan Systems verifies the token Google issues and stores only Google's unique account identifier, which serves as your user ID, together with your session status and expiry. We do not store your name, email address, or password. Sessions expire after 30 days.
GDPR Note: The account identifier is personal data under the GDPR. Otan Systems processes it solely on the legal basis of performance of contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
3.2 Server-Side Account Record

Otan Systems keeps one minimal record per account in a managed cloud database. It contains: your user ID, your Azat API key, your OpenRouter API key (stored encrypted, see Section 5), and your session status and expiry time.

Important: This record contains no conversation content. It is kept until you delete your keys or ask us to delete your account.
3.3 Local-First Conversation Storage

All conversation histories, system prompts, configuration contexts, notes, and active project data are stored strictly on your local device. Apart from the account record above, Otan Systems operates no server-side databases that log, sync, or retain your conversation data.

Important: Because your data resides entirely on your device, we are unable to recover, restore, or export it if you clear your browser storage or lose your device. To ensure complete erasure, clear your browser's site data for the Azat AI domain — clearing the cache alone may not be sufficient.

Section 4
AI Inference Pipeline (Third-Party Services)

To generate responses, your live prompts must be transmitted to external infrastructure. This is necessary for the service to function.

Step 1 · Your Device

Prompt Composed Locally

Your message is composed on-device. No data is sent until you submit a query.

Step 2 · Azat Gateway

Azat Backend (Otan Systems)

Your prompt is sent over an encrypted connection to the Azat backend. A search over our knowledge base adds relevant reference material, and Azat's system instructions are attached. Your Azat key is then exchanged for your own stored OpenRouter key. Otan Systems does not store the content of your prompt.

Step 3 · Third-Party Gateway

OpenRouter (Third-Party Service)

The enriched prompt is forwarded to OpenRouter using your own API key, under your own OpenRouter account. OpenRouter's terms, its privacy policy, and your account's data-handling settings govern what it retains. Responses are generated by the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model.

Step 4 · Inference

Inference Provider (e.g. Novita AI)

OpenRouter routes your request to a model host such as Novita AI. Each provider handles data under its own terms. You can review and restrict provider routing in your OpenRouter account settings.

Step 5 · Response

Response Filtered, Returned & Discarded

The response passes through a content filter that masks profane words and is then returned to you. It exists in our processing memory only for the duration of the active request and is not persistently stored by Otan Systems.

Infrastructure Logging: Hosting providers and API gateways may passively generate transient security logs (e.g. for rate-limiting and DDoS protection). These are not controlled by Otan Systems and are subject to providers' own retention policies.

Section 5
Your API Key & Billing

Azat AI does not sell AI usage. You connect your own OpenRouter account by saving your OpenRouter API key once in the dashboard, and Azat AI issues you its own Azat key in return.

Billing
Directly with OpenRouter
Usage is charged to your OpenRouter account. Otan Systems never receives payment from you for AI usage.
Key Handling
Encrypted at Rest
Your OpenRouter key is used only to forward your own requests to OpenRouter.
5.1 Your Control

You can delete your stored keys at any time from the dashboard, or by writing to otan-systems@proton.me. To stop all use of your OpenRouter key immediately, you can also revoke it in your OpenRouter account.

Responsibility Notice: You are solely responsible for the costs, rate limits, and security of your OpenRouter account. If you suspect your key has been exposed, revoke it at OpenRouter immediately and delete it from Azat AI. Otan Systems does not process payments, hold funds, or issue refunds. Billing questions and disputes must be directed to OpenRouter.

Section 7
Technical Security

We protect live data sessions using the following safeguards:

📦

Containerised Isolation

Application logic and routing proxies are containerised via secure Podman environments.

🔒

TLS 1.3 Encryption

All network data is transmitted over TLS 1.3, the latest and most secure transport standard.

✅

PASETO Tokens

Active sessions are validated using tamper-proof PASETO tokens to prevent client-side manipulation.

🔐

Encrypted Key Storage

Your OpenRouter API key is encrypted at rest and is not shown again after you save it.
