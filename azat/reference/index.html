Section 1
Core Philosophy & Architecture

This Privacy Policy governs the data practices of Azat AI, a desktop application for systems and embedded programming operated by Otan Systems. Azat AI works on a bring-your-own-key basis: you enter your own API key, and the application sends requests directly from your device to the provider you configure. Otan Systems operates no server that receives your prompts, keys, or chats.

Architecture
Direct Connection
Requests go from your device straight to the provider you choose. No Otan Systems server sits in between.
Data Sales
Never
We do not sell, rent, or trade user information with any third party.
Guiding Principle
Local by Default
Chats, keys, and the documentation base stay on your device.

By installing and using Azat AI, you acknowledge and agree to the data practices outlined in this Policy.

Section 2
Data We Do Not Collect

Azat AI sends nothing to Otan Systems. The following data is never collected or stored by us:

  • Prompts, model responses, or chat history
  • API keys
  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account identifiers (Azat AI has no accounts and no sign-in)
  • User passwords of any kind
  • Payment card, bank, or crypto wallet data (Azat AI does not process payments)
  • Identity documents or biometric data
  • Passports or driver's licences (Zero KYC)
  • Usage analytics, behavioural tracking, telemetry, or crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers and tracking cookies of any type

Section 3
Local Data & API Keys
3.1 Local Chat Storage

Each chat (up to 9) is stored on your device as two plain JSON files, {chat_name}_input.json and {chat_name}_output.json, in the application's data folder. They contain the original text of your messages and of the model's replies, including code. Otan Systems cannot access, recover, or export these files.

Important: Chat files are not encrypted by Azat AI and are saved exactly as written, without masking. Anyone with access to your user account on the device can read them. To erase a chat completely, delete its files. If you delete them or lose your device, the data cannot be restored.
3.2 API Keys

You can keep up to 3 API keys. Each key has a label (shown only in the interface), the key itself, a Base URL, a Model, and an expiration: 24 hours, 7, 30, or 90 days, or never. The key itself is kept in your operating system's secure storage (Windows Credential Manager; on Linux, the libsecret keyring). Chat files refer to a key only by its slot (first_key, second_key, third_key) and never contain the key.

Expiration: When a key's expiration time passes, it is deleted from your system's secure storage. Chats bound to it remain readable but stay inactive until you select another key.
3.3 Documentation Base

The documentation base is installed together with the application and read from files on your device. The parts you tick in the Usage List are attached to your prompt locally. No search service or index run by Otan Systems is involved.

Section 4
Request Flow (Your Provider)

To generate a response, your prompt must be transmitted to the AI provider you configured. This is necessary for the service to function, and Otan Systems is not part of this path.

Step 1 · Your Device

Prompt Composed Locally

Your message is composed on-device. The application assembles your prompt, the documentation you selected in the Usage List, the last 3 exchanges of the chat, and Azat's system instructions. Nothing is sent until you submit.

Step 2 · Your Provider

Direct Request to the Provider You Configured

The request is sent to the Base URL of the key bound to the chat, using your own key. Otan Systems does not receive a copy. The provider's terms, its privacy policy, and your account settings govern what it retains, and it may pass your request to model hosts under its own terms.

Step 3 · Response

Response Returned to Your Device

The response is shown in the application, with code displayed in a built-in code viewer, and saved in the chat's local files. It is never seen by Otan Systems.

No Masking: Azat AI does not redact or mask anything before sending. Your provider receives the original text of your prompt and attached files, so do not include passwords, tokens, or other secrets in prompts.
Network Logging: Your internet provider, your firewall, and your AI provider's infrastructure may generate their own logs (for example for rate-limiting or abuse protection). These are not controlled by Otan Systems and are subject to their own retention policies.

Section 5
Your API Key & Billing

Azat AI does not sell AI usage. You bring your own API key from the provider of your choice and enter it in the application.

Billing
Directly with Your Provider
Usage is charged to your provider account. Otan Systems never receives payment from you for AI usage.
Key Handling
Stored on Your Device
Your key is kept in your system's secure storage and is used only to send your own requests to the provider you configured.
5.1 Your Control

You can delete a key in the application at any time, or let it expire automatically. To stop all use of a key immediately, also revoke it in your provider account. Otan Systems never receives your key, so we cannot delete or revoke it for you.

Responsibility Notice: You are solely responsible for the costs, balance, rate limits, model capabilities, and security of your API key and provider account. If you suspect your key has been exposed, revoke it with your provider immediately and delete it from Azat AI. Otan Systems does not process payments, hold funds, or issue refunds. Billing questions and disputes must be directed to your provider.

Section 6
Global Rights & Compliance
6.1 EEA Residents — GDPR

No account records, prompts, keys, or chats reach Otan Systems, so there is no personal data from Azat AI for us to access, correct, export, or erase. The data on your device stays under your own control, and data you send to your provider is governed by that provider. If you write to us by email, we use your address and message solely to reply to you.

Otan Systems does not sell or share your personal information, and Azat AI sends no data to Otan Systems. Requests you send to your chosen provider go directly from your device and are governed by that provider's terms.

California residents have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. As we do not sell personal information, no opt-out mechanism is required. Contact us at otan-systems@proton.me for any other requests.

Section 7
Technical Security

Because everything stays on your device, security rests mainly on how your device and your keys are protected:

🔐

Secure Key Storage

Your API key is kept in your operating system's secure storage and is never written to chat files.

⏳

Automatic Key Expiration

A key can expire after 24 hours, 7, 30, or 90 days and is then removed from secure storage.

🔒

Encrypted Connection

Requests go to the Base URL you configured. We recommend an https:// address so that traffic is encrypted in transit.

📁

Protect Local Files

Chat files are plain JSON. Use your operating system's disk encryption and account protection to keep them private.
